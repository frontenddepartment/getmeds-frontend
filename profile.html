Cytarabine 100 MG / 16.7 ML INJECTION

An antimetabolite chemotherapy drug used principally in the treatment of cancers of white blood cells such as acute myeloid leukemia (AML) and non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

Product Image

Cytarabine belongs to a general group of medicines known as antimetabolites. It is used to treat some types of cancer of the blood (leukemia). Cytarabine works by interfering with the growth of cancer cells, which are eventually destroyed by the body.

  • Effective in acutely inducing remission.
  • High concentration formulation.

This drug is administered by a healthcare professional in a clinical or hospital setting. The dosage and schedule will be determined by your doctor.

Never attempt to self-administer unless explicitly instructed.

  • Tell your doctor if you have ever had an unusual or allergic reaction to cytarabine.
  • Inform your doctor if you have liver or kidney disease.
