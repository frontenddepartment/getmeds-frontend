## 1. Who Getmeds Is

Getmeds is a global pharmaceutical company based in the Philippines, **established in 2020**.
It is licensed by the FDA Philippines as a **wholesaler, importer, distributor, and retail
pharmacy** — which means it can source medicines from abroad, sell them to hospitals and
pharmacies in bulk, and dispense them directly to patients.

Its medicines cover oncology, hematology, anesthesiology, rare diseases, cardiology,
anti-infectives, and essential medicines.

**Is Getmeds registered with FDA Philippines?**
Yes — Getmeds holds a valid License to Operate from FDA Philippines as a pharmaceutical

**Is Getmeds Legit?**
Yes, Getmeds is a global pharmaceutical company located in Las Piñas, Metro Manila, Philippines.
The company operates as a healthcare and pharmaceutical provider serving patients, pharmacies,
hospitals, and healthcare partners through its pharmaceutical distribution and healthcare
solutions.

**Where is Getmeds located in the Philippines?**
Getmeds' head office is located at Unit 301–305, 17 Vatican Building, Vatican Drive, BF Resort
Village, Talon Dos, Las Piñas City, Metro Manila, Philippines. This serves as the company's

**Who is the owner of Getmeds?**
Getmeds was founded and is owned by Naresh Bishnoi.

**What products does Getmeds offer?**
Getmeds distributes a comprehensive portfolio of pharmaceutical products and medicines in the
Philippines across major therapeutic areas, including:

- **Oncology** — targeted therapies, chemotherapies, and supportive cancer care for Filipino cancer patients
- **Hematology** — treatments for leukemia, anemia, coagulation disorders, and blood cancers
- **Cardiology** — therapies for heart, vascular, and cardiometabolic conditions
- **Rare Diseases** — Named-Patient Access Programs and Compassionate Special Permit imports for hard-to-find medicines in the Philippines
- **Antibacterial** — hospital-grade and community antibiotics
- **Anesthesia & Pain Management** — anesthesia agents, analgesics, and perioperative medicines for surgical care
- **Essential Medicines** — WHO-listed first-line therapies and branded generics
- **Biologicals & Vaccines** — cold-chain-managed biologicals and immunization products
- **Medical Devices** — clinical devices and consumables for hospital and ambulatory care
- **Radiology** — contrast agents, imaging consumables, and diagnostic products

Each therapeutic area is supported by Getmeds' dedicated regulatory, supply chain, and patient
access teams operating nationwide across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

**Does Getmeds accept Senior Citizen and PWD IDs for discounts?**
Yes. Getmeds fully complies with the Expanded Senior Citizens Act (Republic Act 9994) and the
Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities (Republic Act 10754). Qualified Senior Citizens and
Persons with Disabilities are entitled to a **20% discount plus VAT exemption** on eligible
prescription medicines purchased through Getmeds pharmacies and direct-to-consumer channels. To
avail of the benefit, simply present a valid Senior Citizen ID or PWD ID together with a valid
prescription at the point of purchase.

## 7. Things Worth Fixing

These came up while reading through every page. All are copy decisions, not technical faults.

**1. The company's age contradicts itself.**
About Us says Getmeds was *"established in 2020."* Careers says *"Getmeds Has Been Present For Over
A Decade In Healthcare."* Both are on the public site. One of them needs to change.

**2. The PDEA licence is described three different ways.**
The home page and Medical Disclaimer say **S-2 to S-5**. About Us says **S-4 and S-5**. Order
Medicines says **S1 to S5**. For a regulatory claim, all three should say the same thing.

**3. Three policy pop-ups can appear empty.**
The Prescription Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy, and Return & Refund Policy pop-ups have no
built-in text — they fetch it when opened. If that fetch fails, a visitor sees an empty legal
document. Only the Return & Refund Policy has a full written-in fallback on its own page.

**4. The Contact page can lose its contact details.**
The contact cards are fetched rather than written into the page. If the fetch fails, the grid shows
only the Social Network card — no phone numbers, no addresses, no emails.

**5. Two phone numbers compete for "general enquiries."**
The footer and most success messages give **+63 919 076 9105**. The partnership form and privacy
contact give **+63 919 076 9103**. Both are legitimate, but a visitor reading two pages sees two
different "main" numbers.

**6. The Global Network menu is mostly placeholders.**
Eight of the eleven entries link nowhere. Anyone clicking them gets nothing, which reads worse than
not listing them yet.
